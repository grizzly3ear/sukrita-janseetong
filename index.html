About Me

The Designer
Behind the Work.

I'm Sukrita Janseetong, a UX/UI Designer with over a year of experience turning complex problems into seamless digital experiences. I thrive at the intersection of research and creativity — grounded in data, moved by empathy, and always chasing that quiet 'aha' moment when design just clicks.

My work spans FinTech platforms, EdTech products, and gaming interfaces. As the sole designer collaborating with PMs, Developers, BAs, and QA teams, I've built the habit of asking the right questions early so solutions hold up long after handoff.

Ready to create something new and make it remarkable.
